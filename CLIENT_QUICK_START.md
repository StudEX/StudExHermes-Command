## Step 1: Create Pipedream Account (5 min)

1. Go to **https://pipedream.com**
2. Click **"Sign Up Free"**
3. Enter email & create password
4. Check your email for verification link
5. Done! ✓

---

## Step 2: Connect AfricaBiz (2 min)

### Option A: One-Click (Easiest!)

Click the link for the workflow you want:

- [Daily Reports → Email](https://pipedream.com/apps/new?app=africabiz&template=daily-agent-report)
- [Deals → Slack](https://pipedream.com/apps/new?app=africabiz&template=deal-alerts-slack)
- [VM Metrics → Google Sheets](https://pipedream.com/apps/new?app=africabiz&template=vm-metrics-sheets)
- [Opportunities → Salesforce](https://pipedream.com/apps/new?app=africabiz&template=opps-crm)
- [Research Updates → Teams/Discord](https://pipedream.com/apps/new?app=africabiz&template=research-discord)

✓ This auto-creates the workflow. Skip to Step 3.

### Option B: Manual Setup

1. In Pipedream, click **"Create Workflow"**
2. Search for **"AfricaBiz"**
3. Click **"Connect"**
4. Paste your API key (from onboarding email)
5. Click **"Test"** to verify
6. Click **"Save"**

---

## Step 3: Customize for Your Needs (3 min)

**For Email Reports:**
- Change recipient email address
- Pick summary time (default: 9:00 AM CAT)

**For Slack Alerts:**
- Select your Slack workspace
- Choose notification channel (#deals, #alerts, etc.)

**For Google Sheets:**
- Click "Select Spreadsheet"
- Choose existing sheet or create new one

**For Salesforce/HubSpot:**
- Connect your CRM account
- Map fields to your setup

**For Teams/Discord:**
- Paste webhook URL from your channel
- Done!

---

## Step 4: Deploy (1 min)

1. Click **"Deploy"** (blue button, top right)
2. See the checkmark? ✓ You're live!
3. Your first event processes automatically

---

## What Happens Next?

### Daily Reports Workflow
- ⏰ Runs every day at 9:15 AM CAT
- 📧 Sends email with overnight opportunities
- 👥 Shows agent performance metrics

### Deal Alerts Workflow
- 🚨 Notifies instantly when deal moves to "Closing"
- 💰 Shows deal value & next steps
- 🔗 Links to dashboard for details

### VM Metrics Workflow
- 📊 Logs performance every 6 hours
- 📈 Tracks CPU, memory, storage usage
- 📉 Historical data for analysis

### CRM Workflow
- 🤝 Auto-creates leads from new opportunities
- 🔗 Links back to AfricaBiz dashboard
- 📞 Ready for sales team to follow up

## Need Help?

### Workflow Not Running?
1. Click **"Monitoring"** tab in Pipedream
2. Look for error messages
3. Common issue: API key incorrect → re-paste from email
4. Still stuck? Email integrations@africabiz.dev

### Want Different Notifications?
- Change email addresses, Slack channels, etc. in workflow
- Click **"Edit"** to modify anytime
- Click **"Deploy"** to save changes

### Want More Workflows?
- Go back to step 2 above
- Choose different template
- Repeat steps 2-4
