# StudEx — From the herd to the horizon

Status: founder-review draft, 12 November 2026. Brand source material, not executable agent policy or permission.

## Our story

Our story starts with the land, the herd and the table. We build around the care that turns raw ingredients into something worth sharing: biltong, meat and coffee. We bring that same care to the tools that help people work.

StudBot is the next chapter: assistants that help a business turn a brief into useful work, with a person responsible for the decisions. The Factory is where that work is prepared, reviewed and delivered. Robo Noir is the proposed bridge into physical experiences and robotics.

The space station is our visual world: a place of exploration, craftsmanship and coordinated work. It does not represent a deployed fleet or claim that robots already fulfil orders.

## One family, distinct offers

- StudEx Meat and biltong: physical products, governed by verified Shopify catalogue and fulfilment information.
- StudEx Coffee: a connected brand experience; inventory, recipes and retail offer require their own verification.
- StudBot: guided agent services with explicit scope, evidence, usage limits and human approval.
- Robo Noir: proposed robotics and hospitality experience; availability is not yet established.

A bundle of agents and biltong needs its own approved pricing, service entitlement, shipping terms and cancellation rules. Do not silently treat food purchases as software subscriptions.

## Voice and design

Warm, direct and precise. Show work before claiming results. Spacious editorial typography and curated imagery from app 3, with obsidian, ivory and restrained gold. Movement belongs to the story and optional exploration; operations, approvals and costs remain readable and still. Respect reduced motion.

The public storefront tells the story and sells approved offers. The private Factory contains customer data, agent conversations, approvals and infrastructure. They share a design language, not access rights.
