## 📱 Participant Experience Flow

### Day 1 (Aug 1)

**Morning:**
1. Participant receives email: "Welcome to StudEx!"
2. Clicks link to AfricaBiz portal
3. Creates account (KYC verification)
4. Receives Tailscale credentials
5. Installed Tailscale VPN (automatic)

**Midday:**
6. Logs into Claude Remote Desktop (claude-desktop.studex.dev)
7. Sees personal VM online & healthy
8. Accesses shared resources via Tailscale tunnel
9. Meets assigned agent (Hermes/OpenClaw/Codex)
10. Reads hackathon/program guidelines

**Afternoon:**
11. Joins team (Rwanda hackathon) OR starts online course (global)
12. Accesses research materials via VM
13. Collaborates with team members (Tailscale secure connection)
14. Agent provides real-time support

**Evening:**
15. Attends Business Club meeting (Rwanda/Nigeria) OR Gaming (SA)
16. Networks with other participants
17. Sees initial partnership matches (AfricaBiz algorithm)
18. Explores marketplace of opportunities
